Healthcare

How computing is transforming healthcare

Authored by Shang Kai Huang LinkedIn GitHub

Introduction

Computing has transformed healthcare from paperwork and manual record-keeping into a more efficient, precise and connected field. By linking doctors across regions and bringing healthcare into people’s homes, computer science is helping save lives and improve the accuracy of diagnoses.

Remote computing in healthcare

Remote computing has helped improve patient outcomes, reduce costs and streamline operations.

It allows rural patients to access healthcare by connecting them with medical professionals without travelling long distances.

Key features

  • Electronic health records
  • AI and clinical decision support
  • Centralised control and monitoring
  • Computer-assisted and robotic surgery
  • Disease surveillance
  • Remote consultations and telehealth
